About Dr.Lillo

I am Assistant Professor in the Department of Physiology, Pharmacology, and Neuroscience at Rutgers University. My focus is ion channels with an emphasis on cardiac and endothelial cells biophysics. Specialties: Patch-clamp electrophysiological recordings, immunostaining, confocal microscopy, co-immunoprecipitation, western blotting, and a lot of expertise using animals models.
