Our Lab team

Pia C Burboa
Research Associate

I’m a biochemist with a Ph.D. in Biological Sciences, specializing in Physiological Sciences and vascular physiology. My research focuses on ion channels and intracellular signaling mechanisms involved in vascular hyperpermeability (post-capillary venules) and vasomotor tone regulation (arterioles) across the peripheral vascular territory (Mouse mesentery vascular network)

Mauricio A Lillo
Assistant Professor

I am an Assistant Professor at Rutgers New Jersey Medical School leading a research program focused on understanding how endothelial cells communicate to regulate vascular function, blood flow, and tissue homeostasis. Scientifically trained in Chile, I completed my PhD there and moved to the United States in 2017, where I advanced through postdoctoral and instructor positions before becoming an Assistant Professor.

Helmuth A Sanchez
Associate Researcher

My professional carrier has been focused in the study of Cx-HCs, trying to understand what conditions induce their opening and how correlate with increase of membrane permeability, loss of electrochemical gradients, and incidence of cell death. The goal of our current research is try to understand what biophysics changes induced by Cx26 mutations could explain phenotypes observed in skin-syndromic deafness disorders.
